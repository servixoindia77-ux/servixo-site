About Servixo

Built by one Sagar local who got tired of unreliable "service guys."

HS

I'm Harsh Sen, and I started Servixo in Sagar because I kept hearing the same complaint from neighbours: nobody could find a plumber or electrician who'd actually show up on time, quote a fair price, and finish the job properly. So we built a single number to call for all of it.

We're still young — just over a year in — but every technician we send is someone we'd call to our own house. The ₹99 visit charge exists so you get an honest, on-site diagnosis before you commit to anything, not a phone-guessed price.

  • Six trades, one phone number to remember
  • Flat, upfront ₹99 visit charge — no hidden quotes
  • Booking & status tracking, not just a phone call
  • Available round the clock, every day of the week
